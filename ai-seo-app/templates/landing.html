Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our SEO analysis platform

What is SEO and why does it matter?

+

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) helps your website rank higher in search results like Google, making it easier for customers to find your business. Our platform identifies what's working and what needs improvement.

How does the AI content optimization work?

+

You can choose from multiple AI providers (OpenRouter, Gemini, Groq, OpenCode Zen) or use Demo Mode. The AI analyzes your content and suggests improvements for keywords, structure, and readability.

What does the SEO health score mean?

+

The score (0-100) is calculated from 10+ technical and content checks including page titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, mobile viewport, and structured data.

Is my website data secure?

+

Yes! We only crawl your public website pages to analyze SEO factors. Your data is stored securely and never shared with third parties.

How often should I run an SEO audit?

+

We recommend running an audit monthly or whenever you make significant content changes. Your dashboard saves analysis history so you can track improvements over time.

Can I use my own AI API key?

+

Absolutely! You can bring your own API key for any supported AI provider. This gives you full control and access to advanced optimization features.
