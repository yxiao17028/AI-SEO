Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use AI SEO Studio.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your name and email address. When you add a website, we store the URL and optional details you provide. We do not store your AI provider API keys — they are used only during the current request and then discarded.

2. How We Use It

We use your data to operate the dashboard, run SEO audits, generate AI recommendations, and save your history. Your email is used for login and account recovery.

3. Data Storage & Security

All data (except API keys) is stored in a SQLite database. Passwords are securely hashed using Werkzeug's hashing utilities. We implement industry-standard practices to protect your information.

4. Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in. No third-party tracking cookies are used. We respect your privacy.

5. Third-Party AI Providers

If you choose to use an external AI provider (OpenRouter, Google, Groq, OpenCode), your content and API key are sent directly to their servers to generate recommendations. We act as a secure middle-layer and do not store your key.

6. Your Rights

You may delete your websites and analysis history at any time via the dashboard. If you wish to delete your entire account, please contact us.

This is a student MVP project. Data is handled with reasonable care for educational purposes.
